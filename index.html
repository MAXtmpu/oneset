How it works

Less time in the gym. More signal in every set.

High-intensity training asks for one slow, controlled set per exercise, continued until the muscle can’t move the weight. Then you rest while your body adapts. Myodose takes care of the timing, the numbers and the schedule.

01

One slow set, timed

Five seconds up, five down, no momentum. The ring fills towards your target time under load and cues each lift and lower.

02

A clear verdict

After each workout, every exercise is compared with last time. Progressed, held steady or recover more, with the rest that follows from it.

03

Progress you can see

A strength score that counts heavier-but-shorter as progress, charts for every exercise, and insights into what actually moves your results.

Built for a busy week

Train properly in the time you actually have.

Every routine is a handful of machine exercises done back to back. The gap between workouts adapts to your results, so you rest as long as your results say you need.

Safe by design

  • Slow reps take momentum out, so the target muscle does the work and the load on your joints stays low and even.
  • A short intro covers setting up machines, breathing and the difference between muscle fatigue and joint pain.
  • Guidance on choosing smooth, well-maintained machines, which make training to failure safe without a spotter.

Myodose gives general training information, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting if you have a health condition.
